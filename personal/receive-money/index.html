Incoming money lands somewhere safe

Payments arrive in safeguarded accounts in your name — screened on the way in, and alerted the moment they land.

  • Balances are held in safeguarded accounts, separate from the money we use to run Teko.
  • Every incoming payment is screened and matched to its reference before it reaches your balance.
  • Only you can see the account details you share, behind biometric unlock and two-factor authentication.
  • You are alerted in the app the moment a payment lands — and if anything looks wrong, we hold it.
