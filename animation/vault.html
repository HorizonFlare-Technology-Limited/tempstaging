Material

Dark non-metal shell with a dense ribbed left side and a bright polished silver chamfered door — the highlight contrast and every gleam come from the live key light and PMREM studio environment; there are no baked reflections or image textures.

Choreography

On first view the open door swings shut while 34 steel-blue bars expand from a condensed lower-left pile to a broad upper-right field around the safe, staggered bar by bar. It plays once; Replay or Space runs it again.

Camera & floor

One fixed three-quarter view, fitted per stage size. A mirrored, dimmed copy of the vault and bars sits below the floor under a soft radial fade — a subtle dark reflection, no render target and no baked floor.
