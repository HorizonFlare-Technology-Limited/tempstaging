One black, five blocks

Every block uses the same near-black glossy metal — #0a0b0d, metalness 0.92, roughness 0.19. The cooler, lighter faces in the reference are lighting, not separate colours: a low blue rim light, a blue under-glow and bright softbox reflections pick out the edges and top faces while the albedo stays black on all five. Each plate is the fig3 thick build (0.26 world units, up from 0.09) with a 1.6 × 1.6 footprint.

Centred Lexend inlays

Each symbol — £ $ € ¥ ₽ — is drawn from assets/fonts/Lexend-Variable.ttf through the FontFace API, turned into a 768² albedo + bevel normal map and inlaid as raised polished metal. The glyph's ink box is centred on the plate in both axes (font ink metrics, not the em box) and sits exactly on the top-face centre — the old viewer-side forward offset is gone. No PNG glyphs and no image assets.

One timeline, two stacked layouts

Both layouts are stacked vertical totems — there is no flat row. Vertical is the plumb fig3 totem; Horizontal keeps the same five horizontally-oriented plates stacked vertically but fans the totem progressively along X. Both share the same gather-and-settle timeline: a loose intro, a bottom-up gather into the flush puck, a 0.19 s tight hold, then a top-down easeOutBack settle onto the rest pose at 2.9 s. It plays once on viewport entry and again on Replay; the layout toggle only re-lays the same five blocks.
