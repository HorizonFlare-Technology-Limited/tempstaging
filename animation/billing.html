Underlit paper stack Loading
Material

The pedestal is the columns deliverable’s own satin column — one createColumns() instance, rescaled to the billing footprint with its top face under the stack and its lower part cropped by the stage. Its satin material is the cloned columns satin, so it inherits the current grain and soft blurred reflection.

Floating text mockup

All seven sheets share one unified abstract text mockup: the same text-line pattern on every sheet, only the ink tone changing (dark on white/gold, light on PCB). It is rendered as a separate layer floating just above each paper surface — not baked into the paper albedo or texture.

Opaque faces

The white and gold paper faces are opaque; only the grey-blue PCB paper stays translucent, so the cool underlight reads through it. Nothing readable is ever painted on any sheet: bars, rules, dot fields and rounded chips only.

Light from below

Each sheet keeps an additive underlight card below it, tinted white, gold or cool by group. On the opaque sheets that light now reads as a halo around the paper edge and as the soft pool on the satin column’s top face.

Choreography

A condensed pile on the right unfolds upward and to the left, staggered sheet by sheet, until every sheet settles flat and face down. It plays once when the stage enters the viewport; Replay or Space runs it again.

Camera & light

One fixed 45° / 19° three-quarter view, fitted to the paper stack plus the support’s visible top band so the paper stack keeps its scale while the satin column runs out of the bottom of the stage. The dark studio uses a soft PMREM environment plus the satin’s captured reflection — no hard floor edge.
