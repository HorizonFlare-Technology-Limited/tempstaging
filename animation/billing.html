Satin column · Letter sheet stack Loading
Support

The platform is the real satin column from src/columns-model.js (satin-large-long), laid horizontal and used whole — no cropping or slicing — with the shared satin material. The sheets float above its top face; nothing intersects it.

Acrylic plates

The top three layers are thick clear acrylic plates (MeshPhysicalMaterial, clearcoat, bright bevelled rim). Each plate carries the supplied doc-icon surface pattern over a dot grid as an alpha layer on its top face — no circuit board and no embossed glyphs any more. The plate is thickened inward: its top face height is unchanged while it grows down to the retired board underside.

Glow paper

The fourth layer is the same wavy white paper plus an orange inner glow: an emissive map that is bright at the sheet border and fades toward the middle (material emission, no light object).

Paper drape

The bottom three layers are matte white paper with a wave (a corner drape plus a travelling ripple); the drape is strongest on the bottom sheet (0.95) and eases toward the top paper (0.52), while the acrylic plates stay perfectly flat. Every sheet is US Letter (aspect 11 / 8.5). Each paper carries thin dark doc-icon line-art (a gutter rule, a title box, three rules, two wide and two small boxes).

Choreography

The layers enter bottom-up, staggered, and then hold. It plays once when the stage enters the viewport; Replay or Space runs it again. Nothing readable is ever painted: dashes, dot fields, rules and outlines only.

Camera & light

One fixed high front-left three-quarter view (−42° / 32°) fitted to the layer stack plus the satin column’s top band, on a black stage; zoom 40–250% by wheel, pinch or the plus / minus buttons. The page has zero light objects: the shared room photograph assets/env/modern-empty-room.jpg is filtered into a PMREM environment by src/env-room.js.
