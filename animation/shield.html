Silhouette

Rounded shoulders (r 0.056 W), straight sides tangentially filleted into tapered lower edges and a wide rounded bottom point — the outer silhouette is a subpixel trace of the reference shield (349 × 461 px, ratio 0.757), extruded with a polished rim and a recessed convex face.

Material & light

Mid-dark gunmetal is the true albedo; the bright rim is a polished bevel, not painted light. One room photograph (assets/env/modern-empty-room.jpg, PMREM image-based environment) supplies every reflection — no scene lights, no baked highlights in any texture.
