Duo composition Loading
Lighting

One key light set to the right of the default 45° camera: the metal highlight reads across the right of each card and the soft shadow falls to the left. The dark card's middle frame is a black metal mirror; the light card keeps its chrome frame. The rig is dimmed so the print, mirror edges and engraved backs stay legible.

Interaction

Drag to rotate; double-click or R resets, F swaps, S toggles the showcase rotation, E toggles the glass-table reflection, L toggles the direct key light.

Provenance

Faces from tekoTransferWeb/public/app, backs from teko2-21/23, gloss from sleek.jpg; derived in-project.
