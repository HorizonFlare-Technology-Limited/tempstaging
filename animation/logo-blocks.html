Two compositions, one canvas

Composition 03 spreads seven silver plates along one 45° line; composition 04 links a logo block to a bank block with dotted gold rays. Toggle, 3 / 4 or ?scene=link swaps studios and snaps to the fitted camera.

Silver mark, gold glow

The TEKO arrow is a machined relief in polished silver and satin graphite, reading through reflected light only. Brand gold is the link’s thin glow ring and ray particles; the mark is never gold-filled.

Real columns platform

The platform is the Columns deliverable’s satin long column laid horizontal — same texture maps and material as columns.html, imported read-only. Every model rests exactly on its top face.
