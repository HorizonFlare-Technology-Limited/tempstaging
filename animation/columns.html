Model

45 real box meshes, no instancing, no runtime scaling: one source pixel is 200 world units (50x; the fine pattern keeps its physical size) (uniform COLUMN_WORLD_SCALE).

Materials

Every column is a real rounded box: its edges are bevelled with a 1 / 1.6 / 2.8 px radius for the small / medium / large footprint (exact footprints, heights and layout unchanged). Plaster is a perfectly smooth liquid-metal mirror with no grain at all. Reeded Metal is the dark rough stone of the reference, its grain carried only by per-point chromatic albedo variation. Satin is a smooth neutral-grey surface with a soft tonal gradient and its blurred reflection.

Studio

One room photograph (assets/env/modern-empty-room.jpg, PMREM image-based environment) lights every column — no scene lights; a neutral floor catches the soft shadows.
